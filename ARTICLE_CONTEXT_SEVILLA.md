# Seville article context

This document provides the manuscript background used by the documentation's
`article_context` page.

## Scope

The Seville case study accompanies the `pyweatherfiles` toolkit and focuses on
real hourly weather-series processing for building-energy analysis.

## Main themes

- Typical Meteorological Year (TMY) generation from multi-year hourly data.
- Conversion between hourly weather series, `.met`, and `.epw` formats.
- Heating and cooling degree-hours analysis from EPW files.
- Multi-year climate-trend analysis over Spanish weather datasets.
- Reproducible notebook-based workflows with version-controlled inputs.

## Practical context

The case study combines Seville and Madrid data to illustrate the package's
end-to-end workflow, from raw hourly data preparation to EPW generation and
trend analysis. It is intended as a companion narrative for the documentation
pages and notebooks rather than as a separate executable script.
